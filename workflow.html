The 5-Phase Cycle

Engineering decisions that preserve your authentic emotion.

Step 01 Pre-Production

Concept & Song Architecture

We start by isolating your track's core emotional spine: one message, one hook, and a defined bpm/key. Before touching expensive outboard gear, we nail the tempo map.

  • Voice memo harmonic deconstruction
  • 2–3 reference tracks analysis (LUFS & tone)
  • Click track and tempo transitions mapping
Step 02 Instrumentation

Sound Design & Arrangement

Arrangement is subtractive: we clear unnecessary clutter so the vocal sits effortlessly in the sonic pocket. We layer analog drum machines, bass synths, and acoustic layers.

  • Song dynamics: Verse vs Chorus dynamic contrast
  • Bass & kick phase relationship locking
  • Space allocation: leaving breathing room for leads
Step 03 Tracking

Vocal & Live Tracking

In our tuned vocal booth, we dial in analog preamps and tube compression to tape. Gain staging is maintained at -18 dBFS nominal to prevent digital harshness.

  • Matched microphone audition (Apollo Twin Unison)
  • Lead takes, doubles, harmonies, and whisper tracks
  • Zero-latency hardware cue mix monitoring
Step 04 Post-Production

Hybrid Analog/Digital Mix

We sum through analog hardware warmth and surgical digital EQ. Sub-bass mono anchoring, vocal formant alignment, and dimensional spatial reverbs bring the mix alive.

  • Multi-bus compression for punch and glue
  • Sibilance de-essing and resonance surgical cleanup
  • A/B car-test and phone speaker translation check
Step 05 Release Prep

Mastering & Distribution Deliverables

The final pass ensures your record competes seamlessly on Spotify, Apple Music, and vinyl pressing without clipping. We deliver broadcast WAV stems and high-res metadata.

Target Loudness -14.0 LUFS integrated for streaming; -9.0 LUFS loud master for club & DJ sets.
True Peak Limiting -1.0 dBFS ceiling prevents inter-sample clipping during lossy AAC/MP3 encoding.
Master Export Stems 24-bit 48kHz WAV, instrumental TV-track, acapella stem, and performance backing.

Artist Guidelines

How to prepare before entering the booth.

Bring your instrumental beat or stems exported as uncompressed 24-bit WAV files (preferably at 44.1kHz or 48kHz). If you only have a single MP3 beat, that is fine too, but make sure the beat is NOT heavily limited or clipped so your vocals have headroom.

We typically record 3–5 solid passes for lead vocals, followed by 2 double tracks for choruses and optional stereo ad-libs. Our engineer comps the best syllables in real time so you don't exhaust your vocal cords.

A rough monitor mix is ready immediately at the end of the session. Full vocal tuning, analog summing, and mastering takes 3–5 business days, including 2 free rounds of revisions for mix balance adjustments.
