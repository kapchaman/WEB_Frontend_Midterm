Recording process

Five steps from voice memo to finished single.

This grid shows a practical workflow that a beginner artist can follow before, during and after a studio session.

Step 01

Idea

Start with a clear message: one emotion, one story and one main hook. Record a quick demo on your phone before changing details.

  • Choose a working title
  • Write the chorus first
  • Collect 2-3 reference tracks
Step 02

Arrangement

Build the song structure so the listener never feels lost. Decide where drums, bass, synths and silence enter.

  • Map intro, verse, chorus and bridge
  • Leave space for vocals
  • Use contrast between sections
Step 03

Recording

Capture strong performances with correct gain staging. Several emotional takes are better than one technically perfect but empty take.

  • Check input levels before recording
  • Record main vocals and doubles
  • Keep the best takes organized
Step 04

Mixing

Balance all elements so the vocal, rhythm and atmosphere support each other. Effects should create depth, not cover problems.

  • Edit timing and clean noise
  • Use EQ, compression and reverb
  • Compare with references quietly
Step 05

Mastering

Prepare the final stereo file for streaming platforms. The master should sound loud enough, but still keep punch and dynamics.

  • Check loudness and true peak
  • Export WAV and MP3 versions
  • Listen on headphones, car speakers and phone
